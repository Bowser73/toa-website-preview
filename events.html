02

First Annual Cornhole Tournament · September 2026

Held September 19, 2026 at Fairmount Park, with activities beginning at 11 AM and cornhole starting at 1 PM. T.O.A. announced community resource and vendor participation from Ladoga Recovery Center, Arise Treatment Center, Intersect’s Hidden in Plain Sight Trailer, Michiana Behavioral Health, and Sloshy Bob’s. The event combined competition with access to information and support.

UPCOMING / OCTOBER 25, 2026

Trunk or Treat Candy Drive

Sunday, October 25 · 4–6 PM at Pipe Creek Roadhouse, 215 E Water St, Alexandria, Indiana. T.O.A. is collecting new, unopened, individually wrapped candy to help fill trunks for the community's Trunk or Treat.

Where to drop off candy

  • Palace Bar: 121 S Main St, Fairmount, IN 46928
  • Pipe Creek Roadhouse: 215 E Water St, Alexandria, IN 46001
  • Jonesboro Legion Post #95: 424 S Main St, Jonesboro, IN 46938
