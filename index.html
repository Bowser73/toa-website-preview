Would you know an overdose if you saw one?

Most people never learn the signs until they need them. Seven to remember:

Pinpoint pupilsThe black centers of the eyes become very small.
Won’t wake upFalling asleep, losing consciousness, or not responding to their name or a firm rub.
Slow or no breathingBreathing is weak, very slow, or has stopped.
Choking or gurglingSnoring-like, gurgling, or choking sounds.
Limp bodyArms and legs are loose and heavy.
Cold, clammy skinSkin feels cool and damp to the touch.
Blue or gray lipsDiscolored skin, especially on the lips and fingernails.
See any of these?Call 911, give naloxone if you have it, and stay with them.
