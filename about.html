Our story

Started in June. Already showing up.

T.O.A. began on June 29, 2026 in Summitville, Indiana, with one goal: a safe place for teens affected by addiction.

Recovery help is built for adults.

When T.O.A.’s founders looked for help for teens, they found programs designed around grown-ups. Teens who are struggling with, living with, or experiencing addiction need a place that’s theirs.

“There are SEVERAL recovery places out there for adults! NOT SO MUCH FOR OUR TEENS!”

Brandy Branham, T.O.A. president

What T.O.A. stands for

Teen Overdose Awareness. In practice, that means three things.

Awareness

Making sure parents, teens and neighbors know the signs of an overdose and what to do in the first minutes.

Education without shame

Honest, calm conversations about counterfeit pills, fentanyl and asking for help, with facts instead of fear.

A place to go

Raising money and searching for a building to open a teen center. See the plan

The first five months

T.O.A. has been out in the community nearly every month since it started.

  1. June 29T.O.A. starts as a community group in Summitville.
  2. AugustThe Third Annual T.O.A. Benefit Ride rolls out of Pipe Creek Roadhouse in Alexandria.
  3. September 19The First Annual Cornhole Tournament brings recovery organizations to Fairmount Park.
  4. October 25About 250 people come to the First Annual Trunk or Treat.
  5. NovemberThe Soapy Joe’s fundraiser opens. Order by Dec 29
