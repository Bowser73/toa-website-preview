Knowledge without shame

An overdose can happen to any family. Knowing what to look for, and what to do first, can change how it ends.

T.O.A. shares the warning signs of overdose, how naloxone works, why pills bought outside a pharmacy can contain fentanyl, and how to start a conversation with a teen without a lecture.
